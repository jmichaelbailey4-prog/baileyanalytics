About Bailey Analytics

Bailey Analytics is led by Michael Bailey, VP of Data Analytics at Citizens Bank, where he specializes in turning complex data into clear, actionable insight for leadership teams.

With over seven years of experience building and managing data solutions across financial services, healthcare, and other data-rich environments, Michael focuses on designing systems that make information reliable, accessible, and useful for decision-making. His work includes developing automated reporting, integrating data across platforms, and supporting executive strategy with scalable analytics.

At Citizens Bank, he has led initiatives including loan portfolio analytics, asset quality monitoring, marketing performance tracking, and strategic planning. He has also applied similar approaches in the healthcare space, supporting analytics initiatives with CPESN. Across both industries, his focus remains consistent: building data systems that help organizations understand performance and make better decisions.

His approach combines technical expertise in tools like Power BI, SQL, Python, and API integrations with a practical understanding of how data is used in real-world business environments.

Bailey Analytics extends that approach to clients—helping organizations move beyond static reporting toward systems that enable more informed choices and measurable improvements to the bottom line.
